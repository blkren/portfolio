# Replacing email cancellations with an exit process

**Context:** A UK professional services compliance business serving 3,000 schools and trusts. I joined shortly after it acquired a competitor. Buying runs through tenders, public budgets and trust boards.

**Problem:** Customers cancelled by email, and there was no Customer Success function or reliable retention data.

**What I did:**

- Replaced email cancellations with an exit survey and workflow, rolled out across teams while still optional.
- Led an SLT workshop on the churn data.
- Built churn-risk playbooks and retention steps into service delivery, presented to delivery teams and heads of service.
- Built a churn recovery framework and win-back motion.
- Trained three colleagues, including my direct report, to run retention conversations alone.

**Result:** 82% of cancellations went through the new workflow over ten months, before it becomes the required process in new contract terms. Directors review the data in SLT meetings. Leadership commissioned tiered pricing, new trust offerings and win-back campaigns, and two other group businesses adopted the process. 18% of previously churned revenue was won back over nine months.
