# Health scoring that looks beyond one contact

**Context:** Be Applied, an HR B2B SaaS start-up selling de-biased hiring software. A £600k ARR book of 80+ start-up, mid-market and enterprise accounts, with 20% variable pay tied to a revenue target.

**Problem:** Average health across my book was 6.5 out of 10. Accounts that relied on a single contact were exposed if that person left, and growth signals were easy to miss.

**What I did:**

- Built multi-threading into my health score: an executive sponsor, a champion and two or more admins per account.
- Used health scoring and engagement workflows to spot upsells and risks early.
- Tracked customer news. When a start-up client raised funding, expanded the account with global reporting ahead of their hiring growth.
- Ran every renewal and pricing negotiation from 90 to 120 days out, planned around each client's decision-makers and budget cycle.

**Result:** Average health across my book rose from 6.5 to 8 out of 10. I averaged 122% NRR against a 110% target and hit every quarterly GRR and expansion target.
