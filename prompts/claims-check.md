# Claims check

**Purpose:** A list of every claim on a page that the evidence does not directly support, so I can confirm, correct or remove each one.

**Inputs:** The page to check and `evidence-brief.md`.

**Prompt:**

```
Check every claim on [page] against evidence-brief.md and list any that are not supported. For each one, say where it came from (CV, my answers, or your own inference) and quote the line. Do not change the page until I have answered.
```

**Notes:** Run after any copy change and before every push.
