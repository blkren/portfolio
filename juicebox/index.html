Three case studies

From red to green before renewal

Situation
A Be Applied client's health had dropped to red ahead of renewal. Their HR team was non-technical and needed webhook and API integrations to get full value from the product.
What I did
Guided the team through the integrations, then presented the results to their SLT and trustees before the renewal decision.
Result
The account moved from red to green within eight weeks. The team reported saving about 22 hours a week during peak hiring, and the client renewed and expanded.

Building churn recovery from scratch

Situation
I joined a UK professional services compliance business serving 3,000 schools and trusts just after an acquisition. There was no Customer Success function or reliable retention data, and buying involves tenders, public budgets and trust boards.
What I did
Built a churn recovery framework and win-back motion. Replaced email cancellations with an exit survey and workflow, then led an SLT workshop on the churn data.
Result
18% of previously churned revenue won back over nine months. 82% of cancellations went through the new workflow over ten months. Leadership commissioned tiered pricing, new trust offerings and win-back campaigns, and two other group businesses adopted the process.

Renewing two central government accounts

Situation
Two of my top five accounts at Be Applied were central government departments, with civil service procurement frameworks, multi-level sign-offs and strict compliance requirements. They came to me largely because of my civil service experience.
What I did
Planned each renewal around the decision-makers and budget cycle. Worked with the Senior Product Manager and Head of Engineering to manage their bespoke reporting in Redash.
Result
Both departments renewed and expanded.

My first 90 days at Juicebox

  • Learn the product as a user. Run real searches in Juicebox, and shadow onboarding, renewal and QBR calls across enterprise talent teams, start-up founders, staffing firms and VC talent partners.
  • Know my portfolio. Map each account's usage, renewal date and stakeholders (end users, champions, TA Ops leaders, hiring managers) and flag any that rely on a single contact.
  • Agree what good looks like. Work with the VP of CS on the metrics that matter, including what a customer's first "aha" moment is and the early signals of expansion or churn.
  • Run success plans and QBRs. Build success plans with priority accounts around their hiring goals, and lead ROI-focused reviews ahead of each renewal.
  • Work onboarding, expansion and renewal together. Start renewal conversations early and act on growth signals such as funding rounds, new teams and rising usage.
  • Improve the playbook. Write up what worked, from onboarding to first value through to risk and expansion triggers, and share it with the team.
  • Scale with AI. Build and share AI workflows for repeated CS tasks such as account research and QBR preparation.
