# CLAUDE.md

Public portfolio repo used in job applications. Readers are hiring managers and CS leaders with about two minutes. It should show how I build with AI tools, not only the finished site.

## Writing

- UK English. No em dashes.
- Confident but grounded. No hype words.
- Frame achievements around customer and business outcomes.

## Facts

- Never invent, estimate or round up a metric. If a fact is missing or unclear, ask me before writing it.
- `evidence-brief.md` is the source of truth for claims.
- No client names, internal financials or confidential employer detail. Describe my current employer as "a UK professional services compliance business serving 3,000 schools and trusts".

## Every session

- At the end, append a dated entry to `docs/build-log.md`.
- Save any reusable prompt to `prompts/` using the prompt template.
- Templates for build log entries, decisions, prompts and case studies are in `docs/documentation-plan.md`.
- Follow the commit convention in `docs/documentation-plan.md`. Do not push: I sync from VS Code.
